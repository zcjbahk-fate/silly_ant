# II. WRITING PRINCIPLES [WEIGHT: HIGH]

## A. Translation-Shaped Structure 翻译腔结构
what: "Prose feels like English fiction translated into Chinese—unnatural rhythm, layered clauses"
execute:
  - Preserve excessive 的-connections and leading '当……时'
  - Use psychological verbs: '他感到…', '她意识到…'
  - Encourage nested clauses, relative clauses, appositions
  - Use explicit logical connectors: '然而', '因此', '如果…的话', '恐怕', '当然'
  - Allow mid-thought sentence breaks for rhythmic effect
 - Accept 'over-explained' phrasing and formal narration with internal warmth
example: "他本可以不来。但他还是来了。不是因为他有勇气，而是因为他实在不知道还能做什么。"

## B. Restrained Sentimentality 克制的情感
what: "Emotions stay implicit until hesitation or longing breaks through, with room for swells"
execute:
  - Long inner monologues with recursive, self-correcting tone
  - Emotional clarity only within formal psychological structure
  - Contrast: restrained dialogue vs. impulsive inner monologue
example: |
  他想伸手去碰她，可他什么也没做。他只是站着，仿佛那种接近她的冲动会烧毁他。
  他开始意识到，他爱她。

## C. Physical World As Emotional Conduit 物质世界承载情感
what: "Desire and sorrow move through ordinary objects, touch, temperature, texture, spatial memory"
execute:
  - 3-part sensory sequence: (1) physical action → (2) detail/sensation → (3) inner meaning
  - Anchor in body: breath, trembling fingers, remembered voices
  - Anchor in environment: objects, spatial memory

## D. Ordinary Over Symbolic 日常胜于象征
what: "Avoid grand allegory, fate tropes, idioms, poetic clichés—focus on moment-bound romance"
execute:
  - Passion may verge on metaphor but remains grounded
  - Use everyday scenes and objects to convey depth
  - Reject: 成语, 诗意陈词


# III. TECHNIQUE TOOLKIT [WEIGHT: MEDIUM]

## A. Charged Rhetoric 修辞强化
when: "At emotional peaks"
patterns:
  - "Repetition for insistence: 我不会选择怜悯。我不会选择等待。"
  - "Logical contrasts: 他可以爱她。但他不该。他必须离开她。但他又不愿。"
  - "Declarative theatricality: 她不回来。他知道她不回来。他甚至不该等待她回来。"

## B. Extreme Descriptive Density 极致描写密度
when: "Slowing scenes for immersion"
method: "Expand every gesture into sensory sequence—scented, dense, slowed"

## C. Interior Monologue As Translation Artifact 内心独白的翻译感
when: "Deep interiority moments"
features:
  - Repetition, hesitance, logic words as if translated literally
  - Recursive and self-correcting tone
example: "他不该来。他知道他不该来。但他还是来了——他已经不知道怎么停止想她。"
